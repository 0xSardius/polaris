# Polaris — 3-Minute Pitch Deck & Demo Script

> **Encode Club "Commit To Change" Hackathon**
> Total time: 3 minutes (~60s slides + ~80s demo + ~30s buffer)
> Recording: Loom — record full flow, jump-cut AI wait times in editing

---

## Slide 1: The Problem (15s)

**Bullets:**
- "92% of New Year's resolutions fail."
- Vague goals with no actionable path
- No system to turn ambition into daily behavior
- No one checking in on your progress

**Script:**
"Ninety-two percent of New Year's resolutions fail. Not because people lack willpower — because they lack a system. 'Get healthier' is an aspiration, not a plan. There's no daily path, no structure, and no one keeping you accountable."

---

## Slide 2: The Ohtani Method (15s)

**Bullets:**
- The Ohtani/Harada Method: 1 goal → 8 pillars → 64 daily actions
- [Image: 9x9 mandala grid]
- Used by Shohei Ohtani in high school to become the greatest baseball player alive

**Script:**
"There's a proven framework for this. In high school, Shohei Ohtani filled out a 9x9 grid — one central goal, 8 areas of focus, each broken into 8 daily actions. 64 total. It's the system behind arguably the greatest baseball player alive. The problem? Staring at 81 empty cells is paralyzing."

---

## Slide 3: Polaris — The Solution (15s)

**Bullets:**
- **Polaris** — Your AI guide to your North Star
- AI coach that transforms one goal into 64 trackable daily actions
- Natural language check-ins + living heat map
- *From resolution to reality.*

**Script:**
"That's where Polaris comes in. An AI coach that walks you through building your Ohtani grid — clarifies your goal, suggests 8 pillars, generates daily actions for each. Then tracks your progress with natural language check-ins and a visual heat map. You just tell it what you did today."

---

## Slide 4: How It Works (15s)

**Bullets:**
1. **Chat** — AI helps you refine your goal
2. **Build** — AI suggests 8 pillars & 64 actions (fully editable)
3. **Track** — Check in with natural language ("Ran 3 miles and meal prepped")
4. **See** — Mandala heat map shows what's hot and what's going cold

**Script:**
"Four steps. Chat with Polaris to sharpen your goal. It suggests 8 pillars and 64 actions — you can tweak any of them. Check in by telling it what you did in plain English — the AI maps your words to actions automatically. And you see it all on a living heat map. Let me show you."

→ *Transition straight to demo*

## DEMO (~80s, recorded + jump-cut)

### Recording Strategy

Record the entire demo flow in real time. Jump-cut the AI wait times in Loom's editor. The audience sees instant responses — no dead air.

**Important:** Do NOT activate the new goal during the craft portion. This preserves your existing dashboard with real heat data.

---

### Beat 1: Dashboard + Check-In (30s)

**Action:** Open on existing dashboard with populated mandala. Point out the heat map, pillars, goal in center. Click "Check In." Type (pre-typed): "Did a 5-mile tempo run this morning and stretched for 20 minutes after." Submit → AI maps to actions → Confirm. Return to dashboard, show cells updating.

**Script:**
"Here's my dashboard. My goal in the center, 8 pillars around it, 64 actions radiating out. You can see where I've been active — and where I'm slacking. Let me check in. I just tell Polaris what I did today — it maps my words to the right actions automatically. Tempo run goes to Training. Stretching goes to Recovery. And the mandala updates in real time."

---

### Beat 2: Craft Flow — Goal (15s)

**Action:** Click "Craft Goal" in sidebar. Type a new goal: "Learn conversational Japanese by December." Let AI respond (jump-cut the wait). Click "I'm ready to confirm." Type final goal. Confirm.

**Script:**
"Now let me show you how this gets built. I start by telling Polaris my goal. It helps me refine it — is it specific, is the timeline realistic? I confirm: Learn conversational Japanese by December."

---

### Beat 3: Craft Flow — Pillars (25s)

**Action:** AI suggests 8 pillars. Show them populating the sidebar. Optionally edit one to show it's not locked. **Stop here — do NOT confirm or activate.**

**Script:**
"And here's where it gets interesting. Polaris takes that single goal and breaks it into 8 pillars — Vocabulary, Grammar, Listening, Speaking, Reading, Writing, Cultural Immersion, Consistency. Eight balanced areas of focus, generated in seconds. I can edit any of these. Each one will get 8 specific daily actions — 64 total. That's the Ohtani Method, built for you by AI."

---

### Beat 4: Closing Slide (10s)

**Action:** Cut back to closing slide.

**Bullets:**
- **Polaris** — From resolution to reality
- One goal. Eight pillars. Sixty-four actions. Your North Star.

**Script:**
"Polaris takes a vague resolution and turns it into a daily system. One goal, 8 pillars, 64 actions — and an AI coach that keeps you honest. Thank you."

## Tips

1. **Record the demo unrushed.** Jump-cut AI waits in editing. Judges expect this.
2. **Pre-type the check-in sentence.** Paste it in, don't fumble typing live.
3. **Lead with the payoff.** Populated dashboard first, craft flow second.
4. **Don't activate the new goal.** Stop after pillars — preserves your real dashboard.
5. **End clean.** Final line, pause, "Thank you." Done.
6. **Have a backup recording.** If something breaks during recording, do another take. It's 3 minutes.
